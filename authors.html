Congratulations on the acceptance of your paper to the SLM-Agents Workshop at NeurIPS 2026. Please complete the steps below by their deadlines.

The camera-ready and reserved-ticket deadlines have passed.

Reserved-ticket form

October 3, 2026 (AoE)

Camera-ready deadline

October 4, 2026 (AoE)

Camera-ready version

Upload the final version of your paper as a camera-ready revision on your paper’s OpenReview page by October 4, 2026 (AoE). Log in to OpenReview, open your paper from the Author Console, and submit the camera-ready revision from the paper’s page.

Go to your Author Console

Each paper may use one additional content page in the camera-ready version:

  • Short papers: up to 5 content pages
  • Long papers: up to 7 content pages

Please carefully address and incorporate the relevant comments and feedback from the reviewers in your final version.

Template

All papers must use the NeurIPS 2026 template, available on Overleaf or as an official download. Update your LaTeX preamble as follows:

\usepackage[dblblindworkshop,final]{neurips_2026}
\workshoptitle{SLMs for Agentic Systems, Paris, France, 2026}

Presentation in Paris

The workshop will be held in Paris only, and accepted papers are expected to be presented in person at the Paris venue. See the poster instructions below.

Poster instructions

The SLM-Agents workshop takes place on Sunday, 13 December 2026 at Sorbonne University (Jussieu campus), Paris. Talks are in Amphitheater 34B. Posters are presented in Caves Esclangon, a 5 to 8 minute walk from the amphitheater.

Poster sessions

There are two poster sessions, each with a coffee break:

SessionTimeCoffee
Poster Session A11:30-13:0011:30-12:30
Poster Session B15:45-17:0016:00-17:00

Each session has 40 poster boards. We will email each paper its session (A or B) and board number before the workshop.

Poster format

  • Maximum size: 61 cm wide × 85 cm high, portrait. Please do not print larger. A1 portrait (59.4 × 84.1 cm) fits and is a convenient size to design for.
  • Landscape posters will not fit the boards.
  • Please include the paper title, authors, affiliations, and “SLM-Agents Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026”.

Printing

You can print your poster yourself or use the official NeurIPS printing service:

  • Order deadline: Monday, 30 November 2026, 23:00 CET
  • Pick-up: Sorbonne University Atrium (or Palais des Congrès, Level 2, Hall Ternes), 12 and 13 December, 09:00-17:00

Ordering details are on the NeurIPS 2026 poster page.

On the day

  • Put your poster up no earlier than 30 minutes before your session. Posters put up earlier may be removed.
  • Take your poster down right after your session. Several workshops share the boards during the day.
  • Hang posters only on your assigned board in Caves Esclangon, never on the walls of the amphitheater or poster rooms. A help desk with tools for hanging posters is available in the poster area.
  • At least one author should stay at the poster for the whole session.

Reserved NeurIPS Paris registration tickets

If your paper requires access to one of the workshop’s reserved NeurIPS Paris registration tickets, please complete the form linked in your acceptance email by October 3, 2026 (AoE), so that we can finalize the allocation of the limited reserved tickets.
